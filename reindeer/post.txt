TITLE
What If Santa's Reindeer Were Boys? 🦌 #shorts

DESCRIPTION
Plot twist: Santa's reindeer are all girls 🦌 Boy reindeer drop their antlers in early winter, way before Christmas, but girls keep theirs until spring. So if Santa's team still has antlers on Christmas Eve… it's girls (or teenage boys) 😳 And Rudolph? Always has antlers. Grown-up boys don't. So the most famous reindeer in the world is a girl 🔴 Did you know this? 👇
