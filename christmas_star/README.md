# The Christmas Star Is Coming Back… in 2080 (pixel short)

1080x1920, 60 fps, 37.5 s seamless loop, no voice.

A snowy village under two bright dots. Through the telescope: Jupiter's moons Io, Europa and
Ganymede (really orbiting 1:2:4) play kick, clap and bass as they pass in front of the planet;
Saturn's rings sparkle on the hi-hats. Then Jupiter and Saturn slide together and become one
Christmas star on Silent Night's high note (Franz Gruber, 1818 - public domain).

Facts: the great conjunction of Dec 21, 2020 ("Christmas Star"); the next one as close is in 2080.

```
python3 audio.py audio.wav
python3 video.py christmas_star_2080.mp4 audio.wav
```
