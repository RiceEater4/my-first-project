TITLE
What If Penguins Gave Christmas Gifts? 🐧🎁 #shorts

DESCRIPTION
Pip has a crush on Penny, so he goes looking for the perfect gift… a pebble 🪨 Plot twist: gentoo penguins really do this! They give pebbles to the one they like and build a nest together 🥹 (yes, they steal pebbles from each other too 😂) Every pebble lands on a note of We Wish You a Merry Christmas 🎶 Would you accept a pebble? 👇
