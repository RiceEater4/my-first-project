# What If Penguins Gave Christmas Gifts? (pixel short)

1080x1920, 60 fps, 26.4 s seamless loop, no voice.

A penguin in a Santa hat hunts for the perfect pebble ("nope." "nope."), finds it, gets it stolen
by a belly-sliding thief, finds an even better one and gives it to his favourite penguin. Plot
twist: gentoo penguins really do give pebbles (and steal them). Every pebble of their nest lands
on a note of We Wish You a Merry Christmas (traditional, public domain), played as a bouncy waltz.

```
python3 audio.py audio.wav
python3 video.py what_if_penguins_gave_gifts.mp4 audio.wav
```
Title and description: `post.txt`.
